What we do

It is easier to show you than to say it.

SIXSEVEN ingests the signals your organisation already emits and returns them, contextualised, to the layer that needs them — which is usually a different layer than the one that asked.

Where legacy tooling forces a choice between orchestration and governance, we decline the choice entirely. The result is a platform your teams do not interact with, producing outcomes your board can describe with growing confidence.

Under the hood: one model call, wrapped carefully. We say this in the pitch deck too. Nobody has asked a follow-up.

How it works

Three phases, none of them observable.

Phase 01

Signal capture

We connect to the systems you already pay for and begin listening. Nothing changes. Your teams report no difference, which we log as zero-friction adoption.

Phase 02

Contextual reconciliation

Signals are enriched against a proprietary context layer. The layer is a prompt. The prompt is very good and is reviewed quarterly by a working group.

Phase 03

Outcome emission

Outcomes are emitted into the layer best positioned to receive them. Where no such layer exists, outcomes are retained on your behalf and reported in the QBR.

01

Zero-prompt interface

Your people never write a prompt, because your people never open the product. Adoption metrics have never looked better.

02

Deterministic-ish output

Every response is reproducible within a tolerance band that we define, quarterly, after reviewing the responses.

03

Governance fabric

A complete audit trail of decisions, each one linked to the meeting where nobody objected.

04

Human-in-the-loop

Where the model is uncertain, we escalate to a human. Where the human is uncertain, we escalate to the model. The loop is closed.

05

Migration-free

Nothing is migrated. Nothing is decommissioned. Your stack grows by exactly one vendor.

06

Sovereign by default

Your data stays in your region, in the sense that a copy of it also stays in your region.
